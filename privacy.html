Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy covers the "Palm Warbler Kiawah Publisher" app (the "App"), operated by Future Worth Estates LLC ("we", "us"), which manages the Palm Warbler Kiawah vacation home on Kiawah Island, South Carolina.

What the App does

The App is an internal tool. It publishes photos, videos, captions, and image descriptions (alt text) that we create to our own Instagram account (@palmwarblerkiawah) and our own Facebook Page (Palm Warbler Kiawah), using Meta's official Instagram Graph API and Pages API. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than us logs in to it.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about any other person. It does not read messages, comments, follower lists, or other users' profiles, and it does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

To operate, the App stores only the following about our own accounts, on a private server we control: the access tokens Meta issues for our own Page and Instagram account, our own Page and Instagram account IDs, and a log of the posts we published (post IDs, links, and times).

How we use it

We use these credentials solely to publish our own content to our own accounts and to check Meta's publishing limits for those accounts.

Sharing

We share nothing with third parties. Content we publish is sent only to Meta (Instagram and Facebook) for publication on our own accounts, and is then subject to Meta's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when we disconnect the App. You can ask about or request deletion of any data at any time; see our Data Deletion instructions.

Children

The App is not directed to children and collects no information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
